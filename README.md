# Loop of a Lifetime

**One lifetime. Countless opportunities. Can you get it right this time?**

Loop of a Lifetime is a browser-based idle/incremental game built around the concept of reliving your life. Start over, make different choices, develop your skills, and discover how small decisions can shape an entire lifetime.

Inspired by games such as *Groundhog Life*, *Idle Loops*, and *Increlution*, the project explores a combination of life simulation, incremental progression, and time management.

## Gameplay

Each playthrough represents a lifetime. Players manage their time, pursue education, develop skills, find employment, and make choices that influence their lives.

The central gameplay loop is:

1. **Live** – Manage your time, activities, and resources.
2. **Discover** – Develop skills and unlock new opportunities.
3. **Die** – Reach the end of a lifetime.
4. **Remember** – Carry discoveries forward.
5. **Try Again** – Apply what you've learned to make the next lifetime more successful.

The goal is to make each successive lifetime a little more productive than the last.

## Development

This is an actively developed personal project. Current development focuses on the underlying simulation systems and gameplay mechanics, including:

- Time and schedule management
- Activities and their effects on character attributes
- Skill development and progression
- Education and employment systems
- Job requirements and job acquisition
- Long-term progression across multiple lifetimes

## Technology

- JavaScript (ES Modules)
- HTML
- CSS
- Node.js

The project uses a modular structure, separating game state, activities, calendar calculations, and gameplay systems.
